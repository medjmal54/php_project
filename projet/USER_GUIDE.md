## Getting Started

### First Time Access

1. Open your browser
2. Go to: `http://localhost/projet` (or your server URL)
3. Click "Register" to create a new account
4. Fill in your information and click "Sign Up"
5. You'll be redirected to login
6. Enter your credentials and click "Login"

### Login Page

**Fields:**
- Email: Your registered email address
- Password: Your account password

**Options:**
- "Don't have an account?" → Click to go to registration
- Remember me: Keep yourself logged in

## User Account Management

### Creating an Account

1. On the home page, click **"Register"**
2. Fill in the registration form:
   - **Full Name:** Your complete name
   - **Email:** Valid email address (used for login)
   - **Password:** Strong password (min 8 characters recommended)
   - **Confirm Password:** Re-enter your password
   - **Phone Number:** Optional contact number

3. Click **"Sign Up"**
4. You'll be redirected to login page
5. Enter your credentials

### Security Tips

- ✅ Use a strong password with numbers and special characters
- ✅ Don't share your password with others
- ✅ Use unique email addresses for different accounts
- ✅ Update your password regularly
- ✅ Use "Logout" when done, especially on shared computers

### Logging Out

1. Click your **profile menu** (top right)
2. Select **"Logout"**
3. You'll be returned to the home page

## Browsing Hiking Trips

### Viewing Available Trips

1. Click **"Hiking Trips"** in the main menu
2. See all available hiking trips with:
   - Trip title and image
   - Start and end dates
   - Difficulty level
   - Price
   - Available spots
   - Region/Location

### Search & Filter

#### Search by Keyword
1. Enter search term in **search box**
2. Type trip name, location, or category
3. Press Enter or click search button

#### Filter by Difficulty
1. Click **"Difficulty"** dropdown
2. Select: **Facile** (Easy), **Moyen** (Medium), **Difficile** (Hard)
3. Results update automatically

#### Filter by Region
1. Click **"Region"** dropdown
2. Select your desired region
3. View trips in that area

#### Sort Results
1. Click **"Sort By"** dropdown
2. Options:
   - Newest First
   - Price (Low to High)
   - Price (High to Low)
   - Difficulty
   - Date

### Trip Card Information

Each trip card shows:
- 📸 Trip photo
- 📍 Location and region
- 📅 Date and time
- ⛰️ Difficulty level (Facile/Moyen/Difficile)
- 💰 Price per person
- 👥 Available spots
- ❤️ Heart icon to add to favorites

## Requesting Participation

### Before Requesting

Check trip details:
1. Click on trip to see full details
2. Verify the dates work for you
3. Check difficulty matches your fitness level
4. Read the full description
5. Confirm the price

### Submit Participation Request

**On Trip Details Page:**
1. Click **"Request to Join"** button
2. Confirm your request
3. You'll see: "Request submitted successfully"
4. Check your dashboard for status

### Validation Rules

The system will check:
- ✅ You're logged in
- ✅ You haven't already requested this trip
- ✅ You don't have date conflicts with other accepted trips
- ✅ Trip still has available spots
- ✅ Trip hasn't been cancelled

### If Validation Fails

**"This trip conflicts with your other hikes"**
→ You already have an accepted participation on an overlapping date

**"No available spots"**
→ Trip is at full capacity

**"Already requested"**
→ You've already submitted a request for this trip

## Managing Participations

### View Your Requests

1. Click **"Dashboard"** (user menu)
2. See all your participation requests:
   - **Pending:** Awaiting admin approval
   - **Accepted:** Your spot is confirmed
   - **Rejected:** Admin declined your request

### Understanding Status

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| ⏳ Pending | Awaiting admin review | Check back soon |
| ✅ Accepted | Your spot is confirmed | You're all set to go! |
| ❌ Rejected | Admin declined request | Try another trip |

### Withdraw from Trip

1. Go to your **Dashboard**
2. Find the trip you want to leave
3. Click **"Cancel Participation"**
4. Confirm cancellation
5. Your spot becomes available for others

**Note:** You can only cancel pending or accepted requests. Contact admin if you need to withdraw from an in-progress trip.

## Favorites

### Adding to Favorites

**Method 1: From Trip List**
1. Find the trip you like
2. Click the **heart icon** (❤️) on the trip card
3. Heart turns red/filled

**Method 2: From Trip Details**
1. Open trip details page
2. Click **"Add to Favorites"** button
3. Trip is saved

### Viewing Favorites

1. Click your **profile menu**
2. Select **"My Favorites"**
3. See all saved trips
4. Use same search/filter options as main list

### Removing from Favorites

1. Click the **red heart icon** (❤️) to remove
2. Or go to Favorites page and click remove
3. Trip is removed from your saved list

### Favorite Tips

- ✅ Save trips you're considering for later
- ✅ Compare multiple options before requesting
- ✅ Get inspired by different difficulty levels
- ✅ Plan future trips by reviewing past favorites

## Profile Settings

### Access Profile Settings

1. Click your **profile icon** (top right)
2. Select **"Settings"** or **"Profile"**
3. Edit your information

### Update Personal Information

**Fields:**
- **Full Name:** Edit your name
- **Email:** Your login email (usually not editable)
- **Phone Number:** Contact number for trip organizers
- **Profile Picture:** Upload or change avatar

### Change Profile Picture

1. Click **"Upload Photo"** or **"Change Picture"**
2. Select image from your computer
3. Click **"Upload"**
4. Picture is updated on your profile

**Recommended:**
- Format: JPG, PNG, or GIF
- Size: Under 5MB
- Dimensions: Square (500x500px works great)

### Update Password

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Click **"Change Password"**
3. Enter current password
4. Enter new password
5. Confirm new password
6. Click **"Update"**

**Password Tips:**
- Minimum 8 characters
- Mix of uppercase and lowercase
- Include numbers
- Include special characters (!@#$%)
- Don't reuse old passwords

### Delete Account

1. Scroll to bottom of Settings
2. Click **"Delete Account"**
3. Confirm deletion (irreversible)
4. Your account and data will be removed

## Tips & Best Practices

### For Planning Trips

1. **Start Early**
   - Popular trips fill up quickly
   - Request participation early

2. **Read Descriptions Carefully**
   - Check difficulty vs. your fitness level
   - Review distance and elevation
   - Understand what to bring

3. **Check Dates**
   - Avoid date conflicts
   - Consider time off from work
   - Plan for recovery days after difficult hikes

4. **Review Costs**
   - Budget for trip price
   - Consider transportation
   - Plan for meals and supplies

### For Successful Requests

1. **Complete Your Profile**
   - Add profile picture
   - Include phone number
   - Write a bio (if available)

2. **Check Availability**
   - Request early to ensure spots
   - Have backup trip options
   - Join favorites for planning

3. **Be Respectful**
   - Respond to admin requests for information
   - Withdraw early if plans change
   - Provide feedback after trips

### General Tips

- 📧 Check your email for important updates
- 📱 Keep your phone number current
- 🔐 Log out on shared computers
- 💾 Bookmark your favorite trips page

## FAQ

### Account & Login

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on login page. You'll receive a reset link via email. (Note: Admin must implement this feature)

**Q: Can I change my email address?**
A: Contact the administrator. Email changes require account updates.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Go to Settings → scroll to bottom → "Delete Account". This action is permanent.

---

### Browsing & Searching

**Q: Why can't I see all trips?**
A: Some trips may be:
- Full capacity (all spots taken)
- Cancelled by admin
- Set as private/admin-only
- Archived/completed

**Q: How do I search for trips in a specific region?**
A: Use the "Region" filter dropdown or search by location name.

**Q: Can I sort trips by price?**
A: Yes! Use the "Sort By" dropdown and select "Price (Low to High)" or "Price (High to Low)".

---

### Participation & Requests

**Q: Why was my request rejected?**
A: Possible reasons:
- Trip reached capacity
- Date conflict with existing trip
- Admin policy reasons
- Trip was cancelled

Contact admin for specific details.

**Q: How long does approval take?**
A: Usually within 24-48 hours. Pending requests show in your dashboard.

**Q: Can I request the same trip twice?**
A: No. One active request per person per trip. Cancel your request first if you need to resubmit.

**Q: What if I need to cancel my participation?**
A: Go to Dashboard → Find trip → Click "Cancel Participation". Cancellations are immediate and your spot becomes available.

---

### Favorites

**Q: Do admins see my favorite trips?**
A: No. Your favorites are private to your account.

**Q: Can I share my favorites with friends?**
A: Not directly through the system. Copy the trip link and share manually.

**Q: How many trips can I favorite?**
A: Unlimited! Save as many as you want.

### Profile & Settings

**Q: Is my information secure?**
A: Yes. We use:
- Secure password hashing
- HTTPS encryption (on production)
- Private profile data storage
- No sharing with third parties

**Q: Can I make my profile private?**
A: Admin can set profile visibility options.

**Q: Who can see my profile?**
A: Administrators can view profiles. Other users see limited information.

### Participation Logistics

**Q: What should I bring on a hike?**
A: Check trip description for specific requirements. Generally:
- Water bottle
- Comfortable hiking shoes
- Weather-appropriate clothing
- Snacks
- Sun protection
- Hiking backpack

**Q: What if I'm injured or can't attend?**
A: Cancel your participation immediately so someone else can join. Contact admin if you're already in-progress.

**Q: Is transportation included?**
A: Check trip details. Most trips include bus transportation.

**Q: What time do hikes start?**
A: Check trip details for start time and location.

---

### Technical Issues

**Q: The page is loading slowly**
A: Try:
- Refresh the page (Ctrl+F5)
- Clear browser cache
- Try a different browser
- Check your internet connection

**Q: I see an error message**
A: Contact admin with:
- Exact error message
- What you were doing
- Time it occurred
- Your browser type

**Q: My profile picture won't upload**
A: Check:
- File size (under 5MB)
- File type (JPG, PNG, GIF)
- Browser supports file uploads
- Try different image
